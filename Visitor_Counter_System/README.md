**Visitor Counter System**

The Visitor Counter System uses an Arduino Nano, two IR sensors, and an LCD display to monitor the number of people entering and leaving a space.

- **Entry Detection:** The first IR sensor detects people entering and increments the IN count.
- **Exit Detection:** The second IR sensor detects people leaving and increments the OUT count.
- **Count Calculation:** The Arduino Nano processes the sensor signals and calculates the number of people currently inside.
- **LCD Display:** The LCD displays the total IN count, OUT count, and the current number of people inside.
